# Enclosure

3D-printable **carrier** that holds the FireFish board inside a standard BlueRobotics 2″
(⌀ 50 mm) underwater housing, plus a clamp. Modeled in **Blender 4.0.2**; print in PETG at
100% infill.

## Files

| File                                    | Variant           | Notes                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `firefish_carrier_short.stl`            | short, detailed   | Carrier with connector cut-outs and support to secure the battery against the board. For the ⌀ 50 × 100 mm BlueRobotics enclosure. |
| `firefish_carrier_short_simple.stl`     | short, simplified | Simplified geometry, for the ⌀ 50 × 100 mm BlueRobotics enclosure.                                                                 |
| `firefish_carrier_long_simple.stl`      | long, simplified  | Longer simplified variant, for the ⌀ 50 × 150 mm BlueRobotics enclosure.                                                           |
| `firefish_enclosure_clamp.stl`          | clamp             | Adds a boltsnap attachment point to the BlueRobotics enclosure.                                                                    |
| `firefish_enclosure_mount_short.stl`    | short             | Mount for the ⌀ 50 × 100 mm BlueRobotics enclosure to attach it to 1-inch aluminium square tubing.                                 |
| `firefish_enclosure_mount_long.stl`     | long              | Mount for the ⌀ 50 × 150 mm BlueRobotics enclosure to attach it to 1-inch aluminium square tubing.                                 |
| `firefish_enclosure_blacktip_mount.stl` | short             | Mount for the ⌀ 50 × 100 mm BlueRobotics enclosure to attach directly to a Dive Xtras Blacktip.                                    |

All are binary STL and mate with a standard BlueRobotics 2″ underwater housing.
